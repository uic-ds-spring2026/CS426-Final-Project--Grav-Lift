The design currently implemented is meant to give an idea of where our development is at, although a few key core mechanics are currently missing and are in development. Since our game is gravity and puzzle based, until we get the gravity mechanic working we cannot really show the puzzle design yet and will save that for the alpha build, and for now we worked on implementing some of the things we will need FOR the levels, one such thing being the Automated Security System Turret. The starting area is actually below where you currently spawn to allow space for you to see the mechanics we added. As soon as you spawn in, ignoring the Mechanim models for now, when you enter the 2nd room you have the cubes placed as placeholders for when we decide which design we want to use as our pushable object, and most importantly you will see the Turret in the middle of the room. It currently mostly follows the player as they go around within a certain radius of it, and if it aims at them for long enough it will fire a missile meant to home in on them. It uses a Finite State Machine to switch between an idle, tracking, and firing state. This is two of our AI implementations, as the missiles themselves track using AI as well. To address the elephant in the room, the Player also has a mechanim model with a FSM, making all 3 of the AI/FSM requirements, and since we really didn't need more than one mechanim model for our game, we added 2 models that we are going to debate using as a player skin that show off animations we may use for gravity swapping (those being hard landings and falling animations so we can see which we want to use and what looks good). There was another model we planned to use but it was 100mb and proved difficult to upload to github. In terms of lighting, we have multiple lighting elements in the lower room, which you can access by jumping down a ramp to the first room and a few extra, and there's plenty of textures in each room, on the models, on the cubes, etc.
